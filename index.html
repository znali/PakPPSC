Step-by-Step Deployment Guide

How to Upload This Website & Database to Northwest Registered Agent (250 MB Free Hosting)

This app is engineered with an automatic ./pakppsc-db.json startup loader so it works on Northwest Registered Agent without needing MySQL or complex backend servers.

Step 1: Upload Your Excel (.xlsx) Files Here First

Click Add Excel Files to DB (.xlsx) in the top bar and select all your PPSC past paper workbooks. They will be extracted and locked into your permanent database.

Step 2: Download the 2 Files for Your Website

1. Click Download Master pakppsc-db.json above.
2. Save this webpage code as index.html on your computer.

Step 3: Open Northwest Registered Agent File Manager

Log in to northwestregisteredagent.com → open your Web Hosting / Website dashboard for PakPPSC.com → open File Manager (or cPanel / FTP) and go to the public_html (or www) folder.

Step 4: Upload Both Files into public_html

Upload both index.html and pakppsc-db.json into the exact same folder. When you open https://PakPPSC.com, the site automatically loads pakppsc-db.json!

How to Add More Excel Papers in the Future on PakPPSC.com: Open your website, upload the new .xlsx file(s) (they will merge with your existing papers without deleting anything), click Download Master pakppsc-db.json, and upload the updated pakppsc-db.json to your public_html folder.
